Pharmacy Management System

Uses the shared vertical core (authentication, permissions, ledger, stock, files, bilingual UI).
Tables are prefixed pharm_ so they do not collide with Aponshop or the other two businesses.
Batch and expiry drive stock. Near-expiry stock is sold first.
Deploy this folder on its own domain or subdomain. Do not extract it over aponshop public_html.
Do not point .env at the live aponshop database.
